Celia Point
Named After The origin of the name “Celia Point” is not widely known, but it adds to the charm and mystique of this lesser-known viewpoint.
Birdwatching: The area around Celia Point is known for its birdwatching opportunities. Bird enthusiasts can often spot various species of birds in this serene environment.
Flora and Fauna: The region surrounding Celia Point is rich in flora and fauna. You might encounter diverse plant life and perhaps glimpses of wildlife during your visit.


Echo Point
Echo Point is a well-known and fascinating viewpoint in Matheran
Location: Echo Point is situated approx 1.5 km from the Main Matheran Market
Scenic Beauty: The main attraction of Echo Point is its unique acoustic phenomenon. When you shout or make a loud sound here, it echoes and reverberates through the hills, creating an interesting auditory experience. Aside from the acoustic effect, Echo Point also offers lovely views of the surrounding hills and valleys.
Echo Effect: The point gets its name from the fact that the natural rock formations and the valley’s shape create an echo when you shout or make a loud noise. Visitors often enjoy shouting out their names or other words to hear them echoed back.

Location: King George Point is located about 2 km from Main Market
Scenic Beauty: This viewpoint offers panoramic views of the surrounding hills, valleys, and lush green landscapes.



Seven Points Circle

Alexander Point
Alexander Point is one of the scenic viewpoints in Matheran
Scenic Beauty: This point offers breathtaking panoramic views of the surrounding hills, valleys, and lush green landscapes. It’s particularly known for its stunning vistas of the Garbett Plateau and the Chowk Valley.
Trekking: To reach Alexander Point, you can enjoy a pleasant trek through the forested trails of Matheran. The journey to this viewpoint is as enjoyable as the destination itself.
Alexander Point is undoubtedly a must-visit destination in Matheran for nature enthusiasts, trekkers, and anyone looking to immerse themselves in the natural beauty of this hill station.


Rambaug Point
Location: Rambaug Point is situated within the Matheran hill station at a walking distance of 30 minutes from the main Market

Scenic Beauty: This viewpoint offers picturesque views of the lush greenery, valleys, and surrounding hills. The point is particularly known for its serene and tranquil atmosphere.

Flora and Fauna: Rambaug Point is surrounded by dense vegetation, and the area is home to various species of birds and butterflies. It’s a great place for nature enthusiasts and birdwatchers.

Walking Trail: To reach Rambaug Point, you can take a leisurely walk along the forested paths of Matheran. The walk itself is a delightful experience, with the soothing sounds of nature all around.

Best Time to Visit: Like many other viewpoints in Matheran, the best time to visit Rambaug Point is during the monsoon and post-monsoon seasons when the landscape is lush and vibrant. However, it’s a beautiful place to visit throughout the year.

Rambaug Point is an ideal destination for those seeking a tranquil and serene experience amidst nature. It’s a place where you can unwind, enjoy the fresh mountain air, and immerse yourself in the beauty of Matheran’s landscapes.

Little Chowk Point
Scenic Beauty: This viewpoint offers breathtaking panoramic views of the surrounding hills, valleys, and lush green landscapes. It is named “Little Chowk” because it resembles the larger Chowk Point in terms of its scenic beauty.

Photography: Little Chowk Point is a favorite spot among photographers due to its stunning vistas and the play of light and shadows on the landscape. It’s an excellent place to capture the natural beauty of Matheran.

Walking Trail: To reach Little Chowk Point, you can enjoy a leisurely walk along the forested trails of Matheran. The journey itself is part of the experience, allowing you to immerse yourself in the natural surroundings.

Little Chowk Point is a hidden gem in Matheran, perfect for nature enthusiasts, photographers, and anyone looking to unwind and appreciate the tranquility of the Western Ghats. It’s an ideal spot for relaxation and rejuvenation amidst nature’s beauty.


Chowk Point
Chowk Point is a popular scenic viewpoint in Matheran

Scenic Beauty: This viewpoint offers spectacular panoramic views of the surrounding hills, valleys, and lush green landscapes. It’s known for its stunning vistas of the Sahyadri Range and the nearby villages.

Photography: Chowk Point is a favorite among photographers due to its scenic beauty and the play of light and shadow over the hills, especially during sunrise and sunset.

Serene Ambiance: The point is relatively serene and less crowded compared to some of the other viewpoints in Matheran, making it an ideal spot for those seeking a peaceful escape into nature.

Walking Trail: To reach Chowk Point, you can enjoy a leisurely walk through Matheran’s forested trails. The journey itself is an enjoyable experience as you soak in the sights and sounds of nature.

Chowk Point offers a serene and picturesque experience for nature enthusiasts, trekkers, and anyone looking to connect with the beauty of Matheran’s natural surroundings. It’s a perfect place to relax and enjoy the tranquility of the Western Ghats.


One Tree Hill
One Tree Hill Point is a captivating and renowned viewpoint of Matheran

Location: One Tree Hill Point is located approx 2 km from the main Market .

Prominent Feature: As the name suggests, the highlight of this viewpoint is a solitary Jambhul tree (Syzygium cumini) that stands tall and alone against the backdrop of the lush green hills. This tree is often the subject of photographs due to its uniqueness.

Scenic Beauty: One Tree Hill Point offers breathtaking panoramic views of the surrounding hills, valleys, and dense forests. The sight of the lone tree amidst the natural beauty of Matheran is a captivating sight and a favorite among photographers.

One Tree Hill Point is a must-visit destination in Matheran for nature lovers, trekkers, and anyone seeking a serene and picturesque escape into the Western Ghats’ natural beauty. The sight of the solitary tree against the backdrop of the hills is an iconic image of Matheran and is sure to leave a lasting impression.

Belvedere Point
It’s one of the popular viewpoints in Matheran,

Scenic Beauty: This viewpoint offers mesmerizing panoramic views of the Irshal Gad Fort , Prabal Gad , surrounding hills and The valleys, and lush green landscapes. It’s particularly known for its stunning vistas of the Sahyadri Range.

Sunset Views: Belvedere Point also offers beautiful sunset views. Watching the sun dip below the horizon here is a serene and captivating experience.

Belvedere Point is a must-visit destination in Matheran for nature enthusiasts, trekkers, and those seeking a serene escape into the natural beauty of the Western Ghats.

Honeymoon Point
Scenic Beauty: This viewpoint offers stunning panoramic views of the surrounding hills, valleys, and lush green landscapes. It’s particularly known for its romantic ambiance and is a popular spot for couples.

Romantic Setting: Honeymoon Point is named for its romantic charm, making it a favorite among honeymooners and couples. The lovely vistas and serene atmosphere provide an ideal backdrop for a romantic outing.

Honeymoon Point is an ideal destination for couples and anyone looking to enjoy a romantic and picturesque escape into the natural beauty , Whether you’re on your honeymoon or simply seeking a romantic outing, this viewpoint offers a serene and memorable experience in Matheran.

Malang Point
Location: Malang Point is situated Approx 3.5 km from The main Market

Scenic Beauty: This viewpoint offers panoramic views of the surrounding hills, valleys, and lush green landscapes. It’s known for its serene ambiance and the peaceful atmosphere it provides.

Tranquil Setting: Malang Point is often appreciated for its tranquility and relative seclusion compared to some of the more popular viewpoints in Matheran. It’s an ideal spot for those looking to escape the crowds and immerse themselves in nature.

Malang Point is an ideal destination for those seeking a quiet and serene escape into nature. It’s a place to relax, unwind, and enjoy the tranquility of the Western Ghats. Whether you’re a nature enthusiast or simply looking for a peaceful spot in Matheran, Malang Point provides a serene and picturesque experience.

Coronation Point
Location: Coronation Point is located Approx 3 km from matheran Railway station

Scenic Beauty: This viewpoint offers breathtaking panoramic views of the surrounding hills, valleys, and lush green landscapes. It’s particularly known for its serene ambiance and the beauty of its natural surroundings.

Sunset Views: While not as well-known for sunset views as some other points, Coronation Point can still offer a peaceful atmosphere to watch the sun go down and appreciate the changing colors of the sky.

Coronation Point is a must-visit destination in Matheran for nature enthusiasts, history buffs, and those looking to enjoy the serene ambiance of the Western Ghats. It’s a place to unwind and connect with the tranquility of Matheran’s landscapes while reflecting on its historical significance.
